› About the app

Times Table 400 turns learning multiplication into a space adventure. Every fact you master lights up a star, and every star opens more of the galaxy — from the classic tables up to 20 × 20.

  • Smart missions that mix new facts with quick reviews
  • Spaced repetition so tricky facts truly stick
  • Flash Cards, a dedicated Squares deck, and a timed Hyperspace Sprint
  • A friendly Shiba guide cheering you on — no ads, no accounts
