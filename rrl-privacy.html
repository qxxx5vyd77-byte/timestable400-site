RRL Poster — Privacy Policy

RRL Poster is an internal tool operated by River Road Labs (Matthew Malham). It accesses only River Road Labs' own TikTok account, with that account's authorization, to upload our own videos as drafts.

It collects no data from any other person. Account tokens are stored locally on our own hardware and are never shared or sold. No analytics, no tracking, no third parties.
