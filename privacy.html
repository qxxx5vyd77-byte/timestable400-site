Privacy Policy · Times Table 400

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Your privacy matters, and we've kept this simple because the app is simple: Times Table 400 does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Times Table 400 has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. We do not ask for your name, email, location, or any other personal information.

Your progress

Your stars, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Third parties

There are no third-party services, no ads, and no trackers in the app. Nothing is shared with anyone.

Children's privacy

Times Table 400 is family-friendly and collects no data from anyone, including children. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to disclose or delete on request.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email apiary15@gmail.com.
